01The shape of three years

Four phases. The proof at the front, the scale at the back.

The plan begins with the contained 90-day Foundation the pitch asks for, then opens stage by stage — Banking Proving Ground, Systems Scale, Global Substrate — exactly as the page stages the scale path. Nothing in a later quarter starts until the certified layer beneath it exists. Each phase pushes the firm one band up the maturity ladder. (The phase labels and their 6–9 / 12–18 / 24–36-month ranges are the pitch’s stage names, not exact quarter-spans.)

The discipline

Every quarter’s “How we measure” line is a yes/no — the same evidence discipline as the 90-day proof, extended across three years. A quarter is not “done” because work happened; it is done when its test passes. That is how a roadmap stays honest and how maturity stays a level, not a feeling.

03An honest read of the dates

A roadmap that lies about time loses the room.

The stop-condition

The plan’s dates above are the ambition; these are the dates to defend in the room. The right way to read the ladder is as capability gates with evidence, not calendar promises — the firm reaches L3 when a governed agent is certified in production, whenever that lands.
